# Lytro — Privacy Policy

**Version:** 2026-11-20  
**Takes effect:** 20 November 2026  
**Provider:** Lytro, Chittagong

This policy explains simply what information of yours we collect when you use Lytro, why we collect it, where we keep it and who we share it with.

We want you to know what Lytro does with your information and your customers' information.

This policy applies to Lytro's dashboard, online shops, the Listener App, the API and the related services.

## 1. Who is this policy for?

**Plainly:** If you run a shop on Lytro or work in one as staff, this policy is for you directly. And if you buy from a Lytro shop, we handle your information on that shop's behalf.

**Shop owners and staff**

When you open an account on Lytro or join as staff, we collect and use your account information directly.

**Customers**

When you order from an online shop made with Lytro, your information goes to that shop's owner. The shop owner decides how your information is used. We store and process it as they instruct.

If you have a question about an order, a delivery or a refund, contact the shop concerned first.

**Language**

If the Bangla and English versions of this policy differ in any way, the Bangla version prevails.

## 2. What information do we collect?

**Plainly:** We collect the information needed to run Lytro, complete orders and keep your account secure.

### Shop owners' and staff information

When you open an account or join a shop as staff, we collect:

- Phone number and name.
- Shop name and address.
- Email address, if you give one.
- If you sign in with Google or Facebook, that account's name and email.
- The wallet and bank account details you give for taking payments.
- The sign-in details of the couriers and payment gateways you connect. These are stored encrypted.
- Screenshots uploaded as proof of a payment to Lytro.
- A record of who changed what in the dashboard, and when.

### Your customers' information

When a customer orders, we collect and store the following on the shop's behalf:

- The customer's name and phone number.
- The address for delivering the goods.
- What they ordered.
- Payment transaction IDs and the number the payment was sent from.
- The outcome of a parcel's delivery, return or cancellation.
- Any special instructions or notes given to the shop.
- If the customer opens an account on the shop's website, their saved address.

### Device and usage information

Some information is collected automatically when you use Lytro, for security and to find problems:

- Your device and browser type.
- IP address.
- Sign-in times.
- Reports of any problem or error in the software.

### Information we never ask for

Lytro's payment page never asks for your bKash or Nagad PIN, password or bank card number.

Don't give your secret payment details to anyone.

## 3. How does the Listener App work?

**Plainly:** The Listener App does not send all the SMS on your phone. It only picks out transaction SMS from the wallets set in Lytro and sends them for matching payments.

Using the Listener App, Lytro can automatically match order payments from bKash payment SMS.

### Which SMS are sent?

- The app asks for permission to read SMS.
- At present, the wallet listed in Lytro is bKash.
- When an SMS that looks like a transaction arrives from a listed wallet's number, it is sent to Lytro.
- Wallet sign-in codes, offers and other ordinary SMS stay on your phone.
- Ordinary SMS from any other number are not sent to Lytro.

### What is done with the SMS sent?

The following is taken from the SMS:

- The amount.
- The transaction ID.
- The sender's number.
- The time of the transaction.

These details are then matched with the payment for the order concerned.

The text of the original SMS sent from the Listener App is kept for up to 90 days. After that it is deleted.

### Test Mode

If you turn on Test Mode, receipt-like SMS from unknown numbers may also be sent for 10 minutes, for testing.

No payment is verified or confirmed with these test SMS.

### How to disconnect a phone

- The Listener App is linked only to your own shop.
- You can remove the phone from the dashboard at any time.
- Once the phone is removed, no more SMS are sent to Lytro from it.

### Lytro's own payment checking

When you pay Lytro by bKash, Lytro uses the same Listener App on its own phone to match that payment.

That app reads only the SMS of Lytro's own wallet, not your personal SMS.

## 4. What do we use your information for?

**Plainly:** Your information is used to run Lytro, to make your shop's work easier and to keep the service secure.

We use your information for the following.

### To run your shop

- To create and manage new orders.
- To match payment details to orders.
- To book couriers and report on parcels.
- To send customers SMS about orders and delivery.
- To create invoices.
- To show sales and business figures.

### To check for fake orders

The delivery, return and cancellation history of your own shop's earlier orders is used.

If you connect Steadfast, only the phone number concerned is sent to them to check its delivery record.

This check is not made with other shops' orders or customers' personal information.

### To manage your account and payments

- To send sign-in codes.
- To verify your payments.
- To create invoices.
- To keep track of subscriptions and the balance.
- To send notices when a plan ends or is due for renewal.

### To keep Lytro secure

- To keep to the set usage limits.
- To stop unauthorised access and misuse.
- To find and fix software problems.
- To watch how the servers are working.

### Support and billing

Lytro's support and billing team can see your account, your payments to Lytro and your usage.

But seeing the information inside your dashboard needs your permission. If you give permission and turn on support access, a record of that access is kept.

### Advertising and marketing

- We don't send marketing SMS without your consent.
- We don't sell your personal information.
- We don't use your customers' information to show Lytro's own advertising.

## 5. Who do we share your information with?

**Plainly:** Only the information that is needed is shared with the services that help get your work done.

### Courier companies

If you connect Steadfast, Pathao or RedX, the following is shared with them to deliver the parcel:

- The customer's name.
- Phone number.
- Delivery address.
- Parcel details.
- The cash on delivery amount.

For the fake-order check, only the phone number concerned is sent to Steadfast.

### SMS and email services

To send an SMS, the phone number and the message text go to the SMS gateway.

To send an email, the email service uses your email address and the email's content.

### bKash

If you connect your own bKash merchant gateway, or pay Lytro by bKash, the payment amount and reference details are sent to bKash.

The payment is completed on bKash's own payment page.

### Google Gemini

If you use the feature that reads order details from a Messenger or other message, the pasted message is sent to Google Gemini.

Gemini helps pick out the name, phone number and address from the message.

### Google and Facebook

If you sign in to Lytro with Google or Facebook, the sign-in details needed to verify who you are are exchanged with that service.

### Meta Pixel and Conversions API

If you connect your own Meta Pixel and Conversions API, purchases in your shop may be shared with Meta.

This may include purchase details, the customer's phone number and district. The phone number and district are hashed before they are sent, so that your advertising results can be measured.

This starts only if you connect it yourself.

### Browser notification services

If you turn on push notifications in the dashboard, the browser's own service is used to deliver them. The notification text may be sent there.

### Cloudflare

Cloudflare is used:

- To store and show your shop's photos and videos.
- To store encrypted backups.

### Error reporting and uptime monitoring

Some monitoring services are used to find problems with Lytro and to check that the service is up.

When an error happens, the details needed may be sent. Keys, codes and payment details are hidden in these reports.

### Government authorities

If the law requires us to give any information, the necessary information may be shared with the government authority concerned.

## 6. Where is your information stored?

**Plainly:** Your shop's main database is in Bangladesh. But some photos, backups and information sent to outside services may also be held outside Bangladesh.

### Servers in Bangladesh

- Lytro's main database is kept in a data centre in Bangladesh.
- Each shop's information is stored and handled separately.
- The shop's identity is checked on every database request.

### Services outside Bangladesh

Some information may be processed or stored by services outside Bangladesh. For example:

- Photos, videos and encrypted backups in Cloudflare R2.
- Reading order details from messages with Google Gemini.
- Information for measuring advertising results at Meta.
- Information about problems in error reporting and monitoring services.

When information goes abroad through these services, the permissions, consents and safeguards that applicable law requires will be followed.

### Keeping your information secure

We use several technical measures to keep your information safe:

- Each shop's information is controlled separately.
- The shop's identity is checked on every request to the database.
- Secure cookies are used for sign-in sessions.
- Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored hashed.
- Courier and payment gateway sign-in details are stored encrypted.

## 7. How long do we keep your information?

**Plainly:** Not all information is kept for the same time. How long it is kept depends on how long each kind of information is needed.

| Kind of information                            | How long it is kept          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Orders, payments and invoices                  | While the shop is active     |
| Activity log                                   | 30, 90 or 365 days, by plan  |
| Money-related activity records                 | 24 months                    |
| The Listener App's original SMS                | 90 days                      |
| SMS or updates sent by couriers                | 90 days                      |
| bKash's answers when verifying payments        | 90 days                      |
| Screenshots given as proof of payment to Lytro | 24 months                    |
| Ordinary server logs                           | 14 days                      |
| Backup copies of deleted information           | Up to 30 days after deletion |

### When a shop is deleted

When a shop is deleted, the process of erasing its business information starts.

This includes orders, customers, payments, photos, videos and other related information.

But the record of payments to Lytro and the invoices stay on your main account.

Deleted information held in backups is also erased within the set time.

Where applicable law requires some information to be kept longer, the legal retention period applies to it.

### Shop owners

You can:

- Export your shop's information from the dashboard.
- Ask for wrong account information to be corrected.
- Ask for your shop's information to be deleted.
- Close a pay-as-you-go shop yourself.

Send requests to delete data to:

support@lytro.app

### Customers

To do so:

- You can contact the shop concerned.
- Or you can write to us at support@lytro.app.

If you ask us, we will contact the shop concerned and help resolve it.

Once open orders are complete, and if there is no legal duty to keep it, on a request to delete:

- Your name.
- Phone number.
- Delivery address.
- Personal notes.
- Your account and address saved on the shop's website.

are deleted, or made anonymous where needed.

The total sales amounts, product details and the thana information the shop needs for its accounts may be kept.

### Staff

The shop owner can remove you from their shop's staff list at any time.

The record of work you did earlier may stay in the activity log. Your name may stay there too, so that the shop's work history is kept correctly.

If you have a question or request about how your information is used, you can contact us.

## 9. What cookies do we use?

**Plainly:** Lytro uses cookies to keep you signed in and to remember some of your preferences.

We use the following cookies:

### Sign-in cookie

A secure cookie is used to keep you signed in to the dashboard and online shops.

Each cookie works only for its own website.

### Language cookie

A cookie is used on lytro.app and on shops' websites to remember whether you chose Bangla or English.

### Delivery area cookie

When you choose a delivery area on a shop's website, a cookie remembers it. This lets the right delivery charge be shown.

### Cart and recent searches

Your cart and recent searches are kept in your own browser.

### Advertising cookies

Lytro does not use advertising cookies of its own.

But if a shop owner adds a Meta Pixel to their product landing page, Meta's cookies may be used there.

This is the shop owner's decision. Telling customers on that page about it is also the shop owner's responsibility.
