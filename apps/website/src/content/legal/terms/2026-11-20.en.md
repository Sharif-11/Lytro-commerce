## 4. When a plan ends or you close your shop

**Plainly:** When a plan ends, your shop is not deleted straight away. You get extra time to export your data or renew your plan.

### When a fixed-term plan ends

After your paid term ends, your shop's state changes step by step.

**Days 0–3: grace period**

- Your shop keeps working as before.
- The dashboard shows how much time is left to renew your plan.

**Days 3–7: view only**

- You and your staff can see the information in the dashboard.
- You can export data, buy a new plan and change passwords.
- You cannot create, change or delete anything.
- Your online shop, payment page, API and Listener are closed.

**Days 7–15: locked**

- Only the shop owner can sign in.
- Signing in is for renewing the plan or exporting data.

**Days 15–60: archived**

- Normal use of the dashboard is closed.
- You can still buy a new plan and export data.
- All of your shop's business information is kept.

**After 60 days: the shop's data is deleted**

After this, the shop's business information is deleted. For example:

- Products and product photos.
- Orders and customer information.
- Payment records.
- Media files.
- Courier and payment gateway sign-in details.
- API keys and other settings.
- Employees' accounts.

But your main Lytro account, the record of your payments to Lytro, your invoices and your balance remain.

### Notice before data is deleted

- You will be notified 15 days and 3 days before the deletion.
- The notice is sent in the dashboard, by email and by SMS.
- No money is taken from your balance to send these notices.
- If you buy a plan or turn on Pay-as-you-go at any time before the data is deleted, your shop goes back to how it was.

### When a Pay-as-you-go shop is inactive

Pay-as-you-go has no paid term, so there is no separate grace period.

- If there is no order fee, product fee or balance top-up for 30 days in a row, the shop is archived.
- You will be sent a notice 7 days before it is archived, that is, on the 23rd day of inactivity.
- After it is archived, the data is kept for another 60 days.
- After that, the shop's business information is deleted under the same rules, and two notices are sent before the deletion.

You can also close a Pay-as-you-go shop yourself. In that case the shop is archived straight away and its data is deleted after 60 days.

During these periods, you pay no fee for essential SMS sent for signing in to your account or resetting your password.

If any of these time limits change, we will tell you in advance under the rules in section 12.

## 8. The fake-order check

**Plainly:** The fake-order check helps you decide. But the final decision is yours.

This feature checks information from two sources:

1. Your own shop's record of earlier orders from that phone number. For example, how many orders were delivered, how many were returned and how many were cancelled.

2. If you have connected Steadfast, Steadfast's delivery record for that phone number.

- Only the phone number concerned is sent to Steadfast.
- This check is made with your own Steadfast API key.
- No other shop's orders or customer information are used in this check.
- No shop can see another shop's private business information.
- If a fee applies to this service, it is taken from your balance at the published rate.

Don't treat the fake-order check's result as final proof. You decide yourself, based on the information, whether to send the parcel.
