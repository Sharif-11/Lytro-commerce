01 Brand platform

One hand should be enough

A seller already runs the business with a phone in one hand. Every brand decision, from the single-stroke mark to one-sentence messages, follows from making that hand enough.

Positioning

The fastest, simplest way for a Bangladeshi seller to run a real online shop, with orders, payments, couriers and fraud checks already connected, in Bangla, with no monthly fee to start.

Lines

Promise Your shop, in your hand. আপনার দোকান, আপনার হাতে।

Offer Sell today. Pay when you sell. আজই বিক্রি শুরু করুন। বিক্রি হলে তবেই ফি।

We are

  • Dependable. Things happen when the seller taps.
  • Plain-spoken. Short sentences, the seller's own words.
  • Quick. The product and the copy both move fast.
  • Local. Bangla first; bKash, Nagad and Dhaka couriers by name.

We are not

  • Flashy. No promise the product cannot keep.
  • Corporate. No "solutions", no "empower".
  • Rushed. Never skip the number a seller needs to trust.
  • Folksy. No village-fair illustration, no cliché.

02 Logo

An L that ends in a tick

One stroke draws the initial, a check mark and a rising line. It is the order confirmed, the payment matched, the courier booked. Round caps, no fill, never a gradient.

Mark on light · teal-700
Mark on chrome · white
Mark on ink · teal-400
Wordmark
Wordmark, dark

Construction and clear space

  • Grid 64 × 64, path M15 15V49H31L49 29, stroke 10.
  • Clear space is one stroke width on every side.
  • Minimum: mark 16 px, wordmark 72 px wide.
  • Wordmark: the mark is the L, then “ytro” in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, tracking −0.03 em. No separate l.

Never

  • Rotate it, outline it, or change the stroke weight.
  • Add a gradient, a shadow or a circle around it.
  • Pair it with a lightning bolt, a battery, or the Lytronix green.
  • Write a lowercase l between the mark and “ytro”, set the wordmark in title case, or write "Lytro Commerce" to customers.
