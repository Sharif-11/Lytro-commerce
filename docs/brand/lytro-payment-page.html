How to read this. The layout follows the reference page element for element, with the same spacing values its markup uses: a frosted top bar with back and close; on the picker the shop's logo and name with "View Details", three contact buttons, the blue "Mobile Banking" bar, four logo tiles and the light "Pay" bar; on a wallet's screen the logo-and-amount header, the faded yellow note, the wallet-coloured card with the centred transaction-ID box and the INSTRUCTIONS list with yellow highlights and the black Copy button, and the fixed VERIFY TRANSACTION button. Below 640 px the page is full-bleed; from 640 px it is a 672 px white card centred on the page. bKash and Nagad use the official marks from docs/brand/third-party/; Rocket and Upay are text stand-ins until their marks are fetched. "Secured by Lytro", the PIN notice and "Report" are the one line the SRS adds under the card.
